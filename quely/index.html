Questions? We've got answers

What makes Quely cookies different?

They're made in Mallorca with simple recipes and 100% natural olive oil, the way we've been baking since 1853. That's why they're so light and crunchy.

Which one should I try first?

Start with Quelitas, the original. If you love chocolate, go for Quelitos. And for snack time, Quely Merienda.

How do I order online?

Add the products you want to your basket and press Checkout. You can change the quantities at any time before paying.

What about shipping and returns?

You'll see the delivery options, times and costs at checkout, before you pay. If something isn't right with your order, write to us through the contact form.
